FEPA — Faculty Entry Publication Audit
The reproducible audit protocol used for new institutes and re-auditing legacy rows.
1. Audit one institute at a time; maximum batch size is 6 faculty.   2. Establish and source the faculty-entry cutoff first.   3. Build the candidate bibliography, then verify papers individually.   4. Record title, venue, date, type, author order and first-author status.   5. Exclude preprints, workshops, theses, patents and duplicates; do not double-count RA-L/L-CSS conference presentations.   6. Derive totals only after the paper list is verified. Cutoff-year uncertainty is explicitly marked ambiguous rather than silently counted.
